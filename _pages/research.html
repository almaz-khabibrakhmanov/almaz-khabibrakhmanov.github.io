Noncovalent interactions are weak, ubiquitous, and still badly captured by the approximations most simulations rely on. My work approaches them from a single angle: build a minimal quantum model of how electrons respond, use it to ask what van der Waals interactions actually do to electronic structure, and turn the answer into potentials that survive in production simulations. The directions below follow that thread — from the coarse-grained model at its core, through the physics it exposes, to where it runs at scale.
