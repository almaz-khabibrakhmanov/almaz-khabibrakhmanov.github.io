Spring visits to Chicago were becoming another tradition. This workshop gave a
state-of-the-art view of how machine-learning techniques can speed up
electronic-structure calculations and improve their accuracy. I learned a lot
and picked up several ideas for future research. I also connected really well
with some of the participants --- not only in discussing science but in hanging
out beyond conference hours, and I caught up with a few of the IPAM guys again.
