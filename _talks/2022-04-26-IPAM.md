This is one of the highlights of my entire PhD. The IPAM program was
transformative, both scientifically and personally. Imagine bringing 40 bright
minds from all over the world together in one place and letting them live and
work for three months, punctuated by four one-week workshops, for which a few
dozen more join temporarily --- all in LA, on a beautiful campus. Sounds like a
dream, right? And yet that is exactly what happened to me, and I'm deeply
grateful to the organizers and to Alex for the opportunity.

There are far too many wonderful things to recount here, so I'll mention just a
few. Scientifically, my time at IPAM deepened my understanding of the QDO model
and quantum chemistry, let me finish the JPCL paper on its parametrization, and
laid the groundwork for building universal vdW potentials. Outside work, it
still makes me smile to remember the house parties, beach runs with Stefan and
Eloise, cozy bonfire nights on the Pacific coast, and the many hikes and road
trips we took together. I also explored LA a lot on my own and kept a regular
vlog on social media --- a nice exercise in socializing and pushing past my
shyness. I hope this gives a flavour of IPAM, and I invite 
you to scroll through the photo gallery for more.
