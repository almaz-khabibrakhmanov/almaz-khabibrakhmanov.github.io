This was a summer school run under the [DTU Active](https://cls.uni.lu/doctoral-training/) 
programme, which funded my PhD. It brought people together for genuinely interdisciplinary 
discussions of living systems, centred on energy, information and evolution. Although my own
research sits fairly far from these topics, it was instructive to get a pedagogical glimpse
of fields like active matter, cellular metabolism and the origin of life. It was also a pleasure 
to hang out with my DTU peers and meet new people from all over Europe. And the setting in 
Cargèse was simply beyond all expectations --- one of the best conference experiences overall.
