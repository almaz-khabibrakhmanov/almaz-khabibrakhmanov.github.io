I was originally supposed to present a poster, but three days before the
conference I received an email saying a talk slot had opened up --- and I
gratefully took it, since my poster wasn't ready yet. This meeting was a
success and marked the start of what became the biennial "vdW/London
Discussions". It was held in Strasbourg --- a beautiful city with a rich history, 
and I loved strolling around it, taking in the architecture and the
surroundings.
