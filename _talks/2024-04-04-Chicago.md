After the workshop I stayed on for another week, on a research visit to the
groups of two outstanding UChicago professors. I had dozens of discussions and
heard about an enormous range of topics --- from many-body Green's-function
techniques for defects in solids to advanced excited-state quantum-chemistry
methods for molecules. It was also rewarding to give a seminar on my own
research and answer questions from such a diverse audience. I am deeply grateful
to Prof. Giulia Galli and Prof. Laura Gagliardi for welcoming me into their
groups and for their time.
