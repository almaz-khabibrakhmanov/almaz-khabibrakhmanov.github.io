The second edition of the "vdW/London Discussions" was held in Luxembourg --- a
home conference for me, two years on from the Strasbourg opener. It was a
pleasure to meet Prof. Giovanni Bistoni, with whom I had a fruitful exchange
about my work at the poster session. Beyond that, there were many brilliant
talks on topics ranging from machine-learning approaches to nuclear quantum
effects and crystal-structure prediction. I also had a personal mission at this
edition: looking after and assisting Professor Emeritus Katharine L. Hunt throughout the
conference.
