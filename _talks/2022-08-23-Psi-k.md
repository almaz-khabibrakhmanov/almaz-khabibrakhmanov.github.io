It almost felt like a tradition: a yearly August visit to Lausanne for a
conference. Psi-k was much bigger than the previous year's CECAM workshop,
with many excellent talks, especially in materials science. It was cool to
run into some of my old MIPT friends there. We travelled as a big TCP team 
and shared plenty of dinners and walks around the city --- and, of course, 
yet another stroll along Lake Geneva was mandatory. The conference culminated 
in a huge party at a nightclub, reminding people that the Covid restrictions 
are over. It was wonderful to see the community socializing again.
