This reunion was long-awaited for me --- visa issues had forced me to miss the
first one. It was great to be back at IPAM and Lake Arrowhead and to see some
of the nicest people again. Importantly, this is where I first presented what
would become my JACS paper on dispersion-induced density shifts. It was
valuable to get comments from Prof. Kieron Burke and to talk through some of
the details with Prof. Éric Cancès.
