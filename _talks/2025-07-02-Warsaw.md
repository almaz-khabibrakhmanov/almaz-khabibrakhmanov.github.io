The week after WATOC, I travelled on to Poland. This conference proved
intellectually stimulating and productive: we heard many excellent talks on
symmetry-adapted perturbation theory, high-accuracy quantum chemistry and the
latest density-functional developments, each followed by sharp questions and
discussion. I was honoured to receive the best-poster prize --- though I missed
the closing ceremony. It was my second week of conferences in a row and I was
worn out, so I left a day early. The award reached me by post instead.
