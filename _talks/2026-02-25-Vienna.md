I visited Prof. Reinhard J. Maurer's group in Vienna, where we discussed the
group's research directions and the potential for collaboration. I spoke with
all of the group members and gave a seminar on my own work. And of course I did
some sightseeing --- it was such a pleasure to be back in Vienna again.
