This symposium was especially memorable --- here I met my future doctoral
advisor, Prof. Alexandre Tkatchenko, for the first time. The line-up of invited
speakers was genuinely impressive, and the meeting offered a nice snapshot of
the state of the art in computational materials science. Among the many big
names, I still remember Prof. Georg Kresse cheerfully introducing himself as
"Mr. VASP" at the poster session.
