My first time abroad and what a place to start: ICTP in Trieste! I was
thrilled. Although I didn't present anything, I was so impressed by the level of
science that I set myself a goal: to produce work good enough that I could one
day present it at a conference like this. Beyond the great science, the venue
was beautiful and the people were kind and welcoming. So, my first impression of
the international atomistic-modeling community was highly positive.
