My first invited talk --- at a series I have followed from the very start: a contributed talk at the opening edition in Strasbourg, a poster in Luxembourg, and now an invited lecture in Perugia. I spoke about how van der Waals dispersion reshapes the electron density and presented new results for pair densities. It was also nice to see dispersion densities now coming from SAPT in Bartosz Tyrcha's talk. The programme was as broad as ever, from quantum chemistry to scanning probe microscopy and batteries. Most valuable for me were the discussions with Prof. Ángel Martín Pendás on established 2-RDM approaches, and with Dr. Christian Wagner on how experiments could probe dispersion-induced density effects at hybrid interfaces. Many thanks to Prof. Giovanni Bistoni and the organizers for the invitation.
