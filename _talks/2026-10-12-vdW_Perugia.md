Upcoming. I have been invited to speak at the third edition of the vdW/London
Discussions, which returns this October in Perugia. I have followed this series
from the very beginning --- a contributed talk at the opening edition in
Strasbourg, then a poster in Luxembourg two years later --- so coming back to it
as an invited speaker means a great deal to me. The programme also features
plenary lectures by Prof. Martin Head-Gordon, Prof. Sereina Riniker,
Prof. Jean-Pierre Djukic and Prof. Alexandre Tkatchenko. I will write more here
after the meeting.
