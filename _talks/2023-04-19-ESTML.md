A cosy joint retreat of three research groups --- that is exactly how this
conference felt. Cut off from the outside world, people threw themselves into
discussion and networking even more than usual. Beyond that, the setting
offered activities you don't get everywhere: skiing, spotting reindeer, or
discussing science in a Finnish sauna. Sitting beyond the Arctic Circle, Levi
is still the northernmost point I have ever reached. Funnily enough, 
on the way in we nearly lost half our team, who got off the plane at the wrong stop 
--- yes, in northern Finland the planes hop between small airports like buses, so you have
to watch where you step out.
