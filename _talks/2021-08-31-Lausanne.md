This workshop marked a new chapter: the first after I began my PhD in
Luxembourg. It also fell during Covid, so the poster session was held fully
online, although we all seated in the same room (yes, what a strange time to remember). 
The conference itself was excellent and served as a great introduction to intermolecular
interactions and quantum chemistry, shifting my focus from materials to
molecules. Lake Geneva and the mountain views immediately found a place in my
heart. One memorable moment was a 6 a.m. run with Alex and Matteo that turned
out to be 12 km long — my legs were sore for two days! Last but not least, I met
so many wonderful people; this workshop was easily the highlight of my first PhD year.
