This time I attended the DPG Spring Meeting --- the largest physics conference in
Europe. It was smaller than APS but similar in spirit, and, happily, I had a
bigger audience in the room. Giving the talk was nerve-wracking, though: right
before I went on stage my laptop froze and simply wouldn't respond! Luckily,
one of the later speakers offered to swap slots with me and the chair wisely
agreed, so my talk was pushed to the end of the session --- but it happened! The
lesson I took away: always carry a memory stick with a backup of your slides,
just in case.
