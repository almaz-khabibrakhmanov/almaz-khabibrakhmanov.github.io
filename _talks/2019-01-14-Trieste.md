This workshop ran back-to-back with the previous one. Here I picked up many
sampling and structure-prediction techniques, and on the weekend in between I
managed to visit Venice and Ljubljana (see the photos). That gave me 
yet another reason to work hard in academia --- the privilege of travelling 
and seeing the world while sharing your results with the community.
