I registered for Elbrus 2020 at the last minute and didn't regret it: the
first "ski-mountain conference" of my career. I quickly learned
that academics really know how to enjoy life. Elbrus is the highest mountain in
Europe, and I almost died trying to ski down a slope without ever
having learned how --- a lifelong lesson! Luckily, i was not injured, and I'm still 
grateful to a mountain rescuer named Vovchik, who found me sprawled beside 
the piste and taught me to ski in an hour for about €20. It still ranks among 
the best investments I've ever made.
