Another huge scientific reunion --- this time for chemists. I presented a poster,
and the venue was almost a labyrinth: people spent hours trying to track me
down. They finally found me right at the end of the session, so we ended up
staying late, and by the time we wrapped up the discussion there was literally
no one else left in the hall. I guess that's how you know you're discussing
real science! It was a pleasure to meet and talk in person with our colleagues
in the vdW field, Prof. Erin Johnson and Dr. Kyle Bryenton.
