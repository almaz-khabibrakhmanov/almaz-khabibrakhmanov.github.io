Here I presented my JCTC paper on universal vdW potentials for the first time ---
and it was very encouraging to get positive feedback from the audience! A
number of excellent talks on non-covalent interactions, density-functional
theory and quantum chemistry made this one of the most useful and interesting
meetings for me. Among the highlights was a visit to Arnold Schwarzenegger's 
house with Alex and Igor. On the way back to Luxembourg I stopped in
Vienna --- a lovely city that instantly became my favourite.
