During the IPAM program I took a side trip to Chicago for the APS March
Meeting. The scale was simply gigantic: some 10,000 physicists in one place.
It was a fascinating experience, though I felt a little overwhelmed, and
networking at such a huge event turned out to be genuinely hard. Part of the
experience, of course, is a near-empty room for your Friday-morning slot ---
classic. As usual, I made the most of the trip and wandered a fair bit through
the concrete jungle of Chicago. I liked the city a lot and, as it happened,
I celebrated my birthday there too.
